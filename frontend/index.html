Label Printer

Each line auto-detects a family colour (IEC/Cxx → power, CAT/keystone → copper, LC/OM → fibre, else hardware) and pulls a metre length like 1.8m into the chip. Force a chip with primary | chip (e.g. Cage nuts | M6).

Sized for 34 × 90 mm bin slots. Preview is exact — hit Print, choose A4, set margins to None and scale 100% for true size. Cut lines are borders, so they print without needing "Background graphics".
